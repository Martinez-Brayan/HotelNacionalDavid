Frequently Asked Questions

What time is check-in?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM. If you arrive earlier, contact reception for early check-in availability.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, we accept pets in specially designated rooms. An additional fee applies.

Is there parking?

Yes, we have secure and monitored parking available for all our guests at no extra cost.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, the buffet breakfast is included in most of our booking packages.

Can I cancel my reservation?

We accept cancellations up to 48 hours before arrival without penalty.

Is WiFi available?

Yes, high-speed WiFi is available free of charge throughout the hotel for all guests.
